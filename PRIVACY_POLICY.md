# Privacy Policy for I Like Planes

Last updated: July 2026

## Data Collection

I Like Planes does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

### Location Data
- The app requests access to your device's location to show aircraft flying in your vicinity.
- Location data is used **only locally on your device** to query public ADS-B flight tracking APIs (airplanes.live, adsb.lol, adsb.fi, hexdb.io, aviationweather.gov, OpenSky Network).
- Your precise location is **never stored, logged, or transmitted** to any server controlled by this app.
- Location queries are sent to third-party ADS-B APIs only as bounding box parameters (latitude/longitude/radius), which is inherent to how these services operate.

### Third-Party Services
The app communicates with the following external APIs solely for flight tracking purposes:
- **airplanes.live**, **adsb.lol**, **adsb.fi** — real-time aircraft position data
- **hexdb.io** — flight route and airport information
- **aviationweather.gov** — METAR weather data
- **OpenSky Network** — historical flight tracks (optional, requires user credentials)

Each service operates under its own privacy policy.

### No Account Required
The app does not require user accounts, registration, or authentication.

### No Analytics
The app contains no analytics SDKs, crash reporting, or telemetry.
